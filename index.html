Common questions

How is Aristotle different from a traditional consulting firm?
We deliver MBB-grade insight in weeks, not months — then we embed the AI systems and trained team so the capability stays after we leave. Traditional firms ship a deck and a bill; we ship a working system you own.
What does "tied to impact" mean for fees?
Our commercials are structured around the outcome we deliver, not a fixed seven-figure engagement fee. We win when you win.
What size of company do you typically work with?
Mid-market through enterprise — typically $200M–$5B in revenue, in industries where revenue decisions are driven by pricing, geography, or demand complexity.
How quickly can we see results?
Initial findings in under six weeks. Most engagements show measurable revenue impact within the first quarter and compound from there as the embedded system iterates.
Who owns the AI system you build?
You do. Code, models, dashboards, and documentation all live in your environment. We provide ongoing support, but the capability is yours to run.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We work inside your security perimeter where possible, and use enterprise-grade controls otherwise. NDAs and DPAs are standard.
