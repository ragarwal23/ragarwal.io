Q&A

What is AI-enabled consulting?

A new operating model that combines top-tier strategy partners with AI tools that stay inside your stack — so your team can re-run the analysis, not pay for a refresh.

How is this different from McKinsey, BCG, or Bain?

Same caliber of analysis (our team comes from MBB), but tied to outcome-based fees and a durable AI capability handover, not a deck. We're faster, cheaper, and the capability stays.

What's the engagement model?

Diagnostic Scan (2 weeks, no upfront fee) → Transformation Sprint (8–12 weeks, blended fixed + success fee) → Compound (quarterly iterations on the AI tools).

How do your fees work?

No upfront fees on diagnostic scans. Blended fixed + success fees on transformation engagements. Our incentives are aligned with yours.

Who owns the IP we build together?

You. Models, dashboards, and playbooks all live in your tenancy. We hand over keys at the end of the engagement.

Is my data secure?

Yes. SOC 2 Type II controls, with all client data staying in your cloud. We work inside your tenancy — your data never leaves.
